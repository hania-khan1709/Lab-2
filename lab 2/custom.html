Explore the Galaxy

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy roughly 100,000 light-years across, home to over 100 billion stars — and our Sun takes about 230 million years to complete one orbit around its center.

About Our Galaxy

The Milky Way is one of hundreds of billions of galaxies in the observable universe. Its four major spiral arms wind around a dense central bulge that houses a supermassive black hole, Sagittarius A*, with a mass over 4 million times that of the Sun. Light from the galaxy's far edge takes tens of thousands of years to reach us, meaning we see much of it as it looked long before human history began. Despite its enormous size, the Milky Way is still on a slow collision course with its neighbor, the Andromeda Galaxy, expected to merge in roughly 4.5 billion years.
